Help Center

Everything you need to know about using ATU PQ Hub. Find guides, FAQs, and direct support options below.

How To Guides

Step by Step Guides

📥
How to Download Past Questions
  1. 1Go to the Past Questions page from the navigation menu
  2. 2Use the search bar or filters to find your course
  3. 3Find the past question card you need
  4. 4Click the red "Download" button on the card
  5. 5The PDF will download automatically to your device
🔍
How to Search and Filter
  1. 1Go to the Past Questions page
  2. 2Type a course name or keyword in the search bar
  3. 3Use the Faculty dropdown to narrow by faculty
  4. 4Use Level and Year filters to refine further
  5. 5Results update instantly: click Reset to clear filters
👤
How to Create an Account
  1. 1Click "Login / Signup" in the top navigation
  2. 2Click "Create Account" or go to signup.html
  3. 3Fill in your full name, ATU email, phone, and password
  4. 4Click "Create Account": you'll get a confirmation
  5. 5Log in and access your personalized dashboard
FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ATU PQ Hub completely free? ▼
Yes! ATU PQ Hub is 100% free for all ATU students. Creating an account is free, downloading past questions is free, and it will always remain free. Moyole's mission is to ensure no student is left behind due to lack of resources.
Do I need an account to download past questions? ▼
You can browse past questions without an account, but creating a free account gives you additional benefits like saving your favourites, tracking your download history, and receiving notifications when new past questions are added for your department.
What if I can't find past questions for my course? ▼
If you can't find a specific past question, you can request it directly via WhatsApp. Message Moyole on 024 560 7110 with your course name, faculty, department, level, and year: and we'll upload it within 24 hours!
Can I contribute a past question I have? ▼
Yes! If you have a past question that isn't on the platform yet, send it to Moyole via WhatsApp or through the "My Requests" section on your dashboard, and the admin team will review and upload it after a quick quality check.
Are the past questions from all ATU faculties? ▼
Yes! We cover all 5 faculties at ATU: Engineering, Built Environment, Applied Sciences, Applied Arts, and Business: 16 departments in total, with more past questions added every week.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
Go to the Login page and click "Forgot Password?" Enter your registered email address and you'll receive a password reset link. If you're still having trouble, contact us on WhatsApp at 055 419 6068 for direct assistance.
Are the past questions authentic ATU exam papers? ▼
Yes! All past questions are sourced from actual ATU examinations and are verified by students who have sat those exams. We do not upload mock questions or questions from other institutions. Quality and authenticity are our top priority.
Who is behind ATU PQ Hub? ▼
ATU PQ Hub is brought to you by Omar Sulley Bin, ATU SRC Part Time Representative for 2026/2027, as part of his commitment to making academic resources easier to access for every student. The platform is here to stay for students, regardless of any office.
