👑 Admins Loading...

To add a new admin: (1) in Supabase go to Authentication, Users, Add user, enter their email and password and switch Auto Confirm ON. (2) Come back here, open All Students, find them (search by the part of their email before the @) and click Make admin. They can then sign in at admin.html with that email and password. Remove an admin with the same button. You cannot remove yourself or the last admin.
