Omar Sulley Bin (Omar Sulley Bin)

Omar Sulley Bin, popularly known as Omar Sulley Bin, is a dedicated student of Accra Technical University (ATU) and a passionate advocate for student development, academic support, and responsible leadership.

As a Part-Time Student, Omar became actively involved in student leadership and development. He joined the ATU Airforce Cadet Corps, where he distinguished himself through discipline, commitment, and hard work, later serving as a Training Officer — contributing to the training, discipline, and development of fellow cadets.

His leadership journey continued when he was appointed to serve on the Faculty of Engineering Electoral Commission, gaining valuable experience in electoral administration, leadership, teamwork, and student representation under the Ernest-Bernard Led Administration.

Academically, Omar completed his HND in Automobile Engineering at ATU and subsequently gained admission to pursue a Bachelor of Technology (BTech) in Automobile Engineering. In 2026, he contested for the position of SRC Part-Time Representative for the 2026/2027 academic year — and won. His vision focused on giving Part-Time Students a stronger voice while improving academic support, welfare, opportunities, and student participation.
