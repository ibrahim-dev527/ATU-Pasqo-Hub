Privacy Policy

How we handle your information on ATU PQ Hub.

Last updated: 2026

1. What We Collect

When you create an ATU PQ Hub account, we collect your full name, Student ID, contact number, faculty, department, and level. We do not collect your email address — your Student ID is used to sign you in instead.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to: give you access to your personal dashboard, keep a record of the past questions you've downloaded, let you send requests to the admin for past questions you can't find, and let the admin (Omar Sulley Bin) respond to those requests.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third party.

4. WhatsApp Contact

If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp (for support or to request a past question), any information you share in that conversation is handled directly between you and our team, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

5. Your Rights

You can view and update your profile information at any time from your dashboard. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us via WhatsApp.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on WhatsApp at 024 560 7110.
